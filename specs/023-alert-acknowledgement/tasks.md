## Phase 3: US-07 Alert acknowledgement ("seen" marker) 🎯 MVP

**Goal**: a nurse marks a patient's open re-triage suggestion as seen from the detail page; every clinician sees "Visto por …" or, once a doctor accepted or dismissed it (US-04), "Atendida por …". The alert stays `open` until the doctor acts; nothing clinical changes.

**Independent test**: `pytest tests/test_alert_ack_api.py tests/test_retriage_api.py` and `npx vitest run src/pages/patient-detail` pass; the manual check in [quickstart.md](quickstart.md) shows the nurse's seen line, the 409 for a second nurse and the doctor's handled line.

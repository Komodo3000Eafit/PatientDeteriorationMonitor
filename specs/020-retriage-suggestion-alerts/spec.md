## Clarifications

### Session 2026-11-21

- Q: When a reading's risk band becomes `high`, how many triage levels more urgent should the suggestion propose? → A: `high` → 2 levels more urgent (`medium` → 1 level); clamped at Triage I.
- Q: Should a crossing into the `low-medium` risk band raise a re-triage suggestion, or only crossings into `medium` or `high`? → A: Only `medium` (severity `medium`, −1 level) and `high` (severity `high`, −2 levels) raise a suggestion; `low` and `low-medium` never do.
- Q: Who may accept or dismiss a re-triage suggestion — any signed-in clinician, or only doctors? → A: Only doctors may accept or dismiss (nurses get 403 and see no action buttons); any signed-in clinician may read a patient's suggestions.
- Q: For the no-duplicate rule, how should the system know the band of the currently open suggestion? → A: The raising risk band is recorded in the open alert's `message`/`reason` text and read back to compare urgency (no new column; no migration). Note: today no code creates any `Alert` row; US-04 is the first to write one, so there is no prior model to match.

### Change request 2026-11-21 (after live testing)

- **CR1 — Open suggestions on the patient list page**: open re-triage suggestions must also be shown at the **top of the principal patient list page** (`/patients`), not only on each patient's detail page, so a clinician sees who needs attention first without opening each patient. It is **display and link only** (each item links to that patient's detail page, where Accept/Dismiss live); no accept/dismiss action on the list. Colors and icons by severity: **`high` → red (critical tokens), `medium` → yellow (warning tokens)**, each with a `lucide-react` icon and a text label (color never the only cue). A new read endpoint `GET /api/suggestions` returns all open suggestions (any signed-in clinician). Adds AC21–AC23, FR-018–FR-020.
- **CR2 — Translatable reason (i18n fix)**: the suggestion **reason** (shown on the detail page) must be rendered from **structured data translated by the frontend**, not a pre-built English string stored in the database. The API's `reason` field becomes a **list of `{parameter, points}`** (parameter code + integer points, the parameters that scored > 0, in `PARAMETERS` order); the frontend builds the reason text from `parameter.*` and `recordReading.points` in `translations.js`, so it switches with the language. The human sentence (band + current/suggested level) in `message` is unchanged. No submitted vital value is ever included. Adds AC24, FR-021; revises FR-004 and the contract `reason` field.

## User Story *(mandatory)*

**Story ID**: US-04

**Type**: Functional (baseline)

**Story**: As a doctor, I want an alert suggesting re-triage to a more urgent level when a patient's NEWS2 risk band crosses the threshold, explaining why, so that I can decide sooner whether to change the triage level.

**Depends on**: TS-03 (tables and migrations: `alerts`, `triage_events`), TS-06 (authentication), TS-07 (clinical scoring module), TS-12 (UI foundation), TS-13 (client routing). All merged. US-03 (stored scores: `reading_scores`) is merged and reused: this story reads the stored score and never recomputes NEWS2.

### Acceptance Criteria

<!-- Each criterion must be testable. qa-tester writes at least one test per criterion. -->

1. **AC1 — A crossing into a more urgent risk band raises a suggestion alert**: **Given** an active patient whose latest stored reading has a risk band more urgent than the band of their previous stored reading (for example `low` → `medium`), **When** the reading is saved, **Then** exactly one open re-triage suggestion alert is stored for that patient, with an alert severity derived from the new risk band (FR-002), a message that names the new risk band, the patient's current triage level and the suggested more urgent triage level, and the reason (which vital signs and points produced the score), all built from the stored score and never recomputed.
2. **AC2 — The app never changes the triage level on its own**: **Given** a re-triage suggestion alert is raised, **When** it is stored, **Then** the patient's `triage_level` is unchanged and no `triage_event` with `source` = `automatic` is created; the triage level changes only through a `manual` triage event created when a clinician accepts the suggestion (AC6).
3. **AC3 — No suggestion when the band does not become more urgent**: **Given** a patient's latest reading, **When** its risk band is the same as or less urgent than the previous reading's band (for example `medium` → `low`, or `medium` → `medium`), **Then** no new re-triage suggestion alert is created for that reading.
4. **AC4 — No duplicate open suggestion**: **Given** a patient who already has an open re-triage suggestion alert whose raising band is recorded in its stored text, **When** a later reading's band is the same as or less urgent than that recorded band, **Then** no second open suggestion alert is created; **When** a later reading crosses into a band strictly more urgent than the recorded band (that is, `medium` → `high`), **Then** a new suggestion alert is created for the more urgent level.
5. **AC5 — The suggested level is more urgent, bounded at the most urgent level**: **Given** a patient with a given current triage level, **When** a re-triage suggestion is raised, **Then** the suggested level is more urgent than the current level (a lower number) by the step the band mapping gives (FR-003), never more urgent than Triage I (level 1), and a patient already at Triage I never receives a suggestion to go more urgent.
6. **AC6 — A doctor accepts a suggestion (manual re-triage)**: **Given** a signed-in **doctor** and an open re-triage suggestion alert, **When** they accept it, **Then** the patient's `triage_level` is set to the suggested level, exactly one `triage_event` is stored with `source` = `manual`, `previous_level` = the level before, `new_level` = the suggested level, `created_by_id` = the acting user, `occurred_at` = the server save time and a `reason` that records the accepted suggestion, the alert's `status` becomes `acknowledged` with `acknowledged_by_id` and `acknowledged_at` set, and all of this is saved together or not at all.
7. **AC7 — A doctor dismisses a suggestion**: **Given** a signed-in **doctor** and an open re-triage suggestion alert, **When** they dismiss it, **Then** the alert's `status` becomes `acknowledged` with `acknowledged_by_id` and `acknowledged_at` set, the patient's `triage_level` is unchanged and no `triage_event` is created.
8. **AC8 — Reading the suggestion alerts**: **Given** any signed-in clinician (doctor or nurse), **When** they ask for a patient's re-triage suggestion alerts, **Then** they get that patient's suggestion alerts newest first, each with its severity, message, reason, current and suggested triage level and status (`open` or `acknowledged`); signed out the answer is 401; an unknown patient code gives 404.
9. **AC9 — Who may read and act**: **Given** a request to read a patient's suggestion alerts, **When** it is sent signed out, **Then** the answer is 401, and **When** it is sent by any signed-in clinician (doctor or nurse), **Then** it is allowed; **Given** a request to accept or dismiss a suggestion, **When** it is sent signed out, **Then** the answer is 401, **When** it is sent by a signed-in **nurse**, **Then** the answer is 403 with a fixed message, and **When** it is sent by a signed-in **doctor**, **Then** it is allowed; nothing is stored on a 401 or 403.
10. **AC10 — Acting on an already acknowledged or unknown alert**: **Given** a request to accept or dismiss an alert, **When** the alert does not exist for that patient, **Then** the answer is 404 with a fixed message; **When** the alert is already `acknowledged`, **Then** the answer is 409 with a fixed message and nothing changes.
11. **AC11 — Discharged patient**: **Given** a reading or an accept/dismiss request for a patient whose `status` is `discharged`, **When** it is processed, **Then** no new suggestion alert is created for a discharged patient and an accept is refused with 409; the patient's existing suggestion alerts stay readable.
12. **AC12 — All or nothing**: **Given** a reading that triggers a suggestion, or an accept that creates a triage event, **When** any part of the write fails (a database error, a scoring read failure, a conflict), **Then** nothing partial is stored: no reading without its handling, no alert without its fields, and no triage event without the alert being acknowledged.
13. **AC13 — Input validation and safe errors**: **Given** any request in this story, **When** the patient code is not in the patient code format or the alert reference is malformed, **Then** the answer is 404 with a fixed message; error answers (401, 404, 409, 422, 503) use fixed, safe messages that reveal no internal details and never echo a submitted value.
14. **AC14 — Suggestion shown on the patient detail area** (CR 2026-11-21: the separate risk-band badge was removed as redundant with the severity chip; the severity is shown as a chip with a colored icon): **Given** a signed-in clinician on the mounted suggestion component for a patient with an open re-triage suggestion, **When** it is shown, **Then** it displays the alert severity as a chip with a colored `lucide-react` icon (`high` red / critical tokens, `medium` yellow / warning tokens) and a dark text label, the current triage level and the suggested triage level as triage level badges, and the reason for each point, all inside a Card; the "Accept" and "Dismiss" actions are shown only to a doctor (a nurse sees the suggestion read-only, with no action buttons); everything shown comes from the alert, the frontend never computes a band, a score or a suggested level.
15. **AC15 — No open suggestion**: **Given** a patient with no open re-triage suggestion, **When** the suggestion component is shown, **Then** it shows a translated "no open re-triage suggestion" message and no action buttons.
16. **AC16 — Accept and dismiss from the UI (doctor)**: **Given** an open suggestion shown to a **doctor**, **When** they choose "Accept", **Then** the component asks the system to accept and, on success, shows the patient's new triage level and that the suggestion was accepted; **When** they choose "Dismiss", **Then** the component asks the system to dismiss and, on success, shows that the suggestion was dismissed; while an action is in progress its buttons are disabled so it cannot be sent twice. A nurse sees no action buttons, so neither action is available to them.
17. **AC17 — Failed action keeps the suggestion**: **Given** a clinician acting on a suggestion, **When** the system answers 404, 409, a server error or no answer (network failure or timeout), **Then** the component shows a fixed, translated error message (never the raw server text), the suggestion stays shown, and the buttons are usable again; on 401 the existing session-expiry handling applies.
18. **AC18 — Spanish by default, English switch**: **Given** the suggestion component, its messages, the band and triage labels and the actions, **When** the language is Spanish (default) or English, **Then** every label, band name, triage level, severity, reason, button and message is shown in that language using glossary terms, and switching language does not clear what is shown.
19. **AC19 — Reason is explainable (constitution Principle V)**: **Given** a re-triage suggestion, **When** its reason is shown, **Then** it names the vital signs and the points that produced the NEWS2 score and risk band (read from the stored explanation entries), so a clinician can understand and challenge the suggestion; the reason never contains a raw score computation done in the UI.
20. **AC20 — Styling and tokens**: **Given** the suggestion component, **When** it is shown, **Then** all styling uses design tokens only (no raw colors outside `tokens.css`), color is never the only cue (each badge keeps its label and icon), any new icon comes from `lucide-react` (D-46), and the existing raw-color and contrast guard tests keep passing.
21. **AC21 — Open suggestions listed on the principal page (CR1)**: **Given** a signed-in clinician on the patient list page (`/patients`), **When** one or more patients have an open re-triage suggestion, **Then** a banner at the top lists them, each row showing the patient code, the alert severity with a color and a `lucide-react` icon (`high` red / critical tokens, `medium` yellow / warning tokens) and a text label, and the suggested triage level; **When** there are no open suggestions, **Then** the banner shows a translated "no open suggestions" message (or renders nothing above the list) and never an error on a normal empty result.
22. **AC22 — List rows link to the patient (CR1)**: **Given** the list-page banner, **When** a clinician selects a listed suggestion, **Then** they are taken to that patient's detail page (`/patients/<code>`), where Accept and Dismiss live; the banner itself offers no accept or dismiss action.
23. **AC23 — All open suggestions endpoint (CR1)**: **Given** a request to `GET /api/suggestions`, **When** it is sent by any signed-in clinician, **Then** it returns every open re-triage suggestion across patients (each with the patient code, severity, suggested level and the translatable reason data), newest first; signed out the answer is 401; the response never includes `patient_id`, `acknowledged_by_id` or the internal machine tag.
24. **AC24 — Reason is translatable structured data (CR2)**: **Given** a re-triage suggestion, **When** its reason is returned by the API and shown on the detail page, **Then** the API `reason` is a list of `{parameter, points}` entries (parameter code and integer points, only parameters scoring > 0, in the standard parameter order), carrying no submitted vital value; **and** the frontend renders the reason text from `parameter.*` and `recordReading.points` in the translations file, so it is shown in Spanish by default and in English after the switch, using glossary terms, with no English parameter code or bare number shown to the user.

### Out of Scope

- The content of the patient list (US-02), the patient detail page body (US-05) and charts (US-06): this story only adds one mounted suggestion component to the patient detail area and its own endpoints.
- Acknowledging arbitrary alerts from a general alert list (US-07): this story acknowledges a suggestion alert only through accept or dismiss on that patient.
- The deterioration-prediction warning (US-08): this story reacts to a risk band crossing on a saved reading, not to a rising trend across readings.
- Creating alerts for anything other than a re-triage suggestion, editing or deleting alerts, or notifying users outside the app.
- Any database migration: the existing `alerts` and `triage_events` tables are used as they are.
- Computing NEWS2, points, the risk band or the suggested level in the frontend; it only displays what the backend stored.
- Changing `triage_events.source` enum values or the `alerts` table shape.

### Edge Cases

- **First ever reading for a patient** (no previous reading to compare): there is no band crossing, so no suggestion is created; the band only "crosses" when a previous stored reading exists with a less urgent band.
- **Band crossing straight to `high`** (for example `low` → `high`): one suggestion is raised at the severity and step the mapping gives for `high`.
- **Patient already at Triage I** (level 1): no suggestion to go more urgent is created, even when the band becomes more urgent (AC5).
- **Suggested level clamps at Triage I**: a step that would go below level 1 is capped at level 1.
- **Open suggestion already at the suggested level**: a later reading at the same or a less urgent band does not create a second suggestion (AC4); a crossing to an even more urgent band does.
- **Band value the mapping does not know** (an unexpected `band`): no suggestion is created and the problem is logged by type only, never shown to a user or stored as an alert.
- **Accept on a patient discharged between showing and accepting**: the accept gets 409 and nothing changes.
- **Accept or dismiss on an alert already acknowledged by someone else**: 409 with a fixed message; nothing changes (AC10).
- **Two accepts of the same open suggestion at the same time**: only the first succeeds; the second sees the alert already acknowledged and gets 409.
- **Session expires while the suggestion is shown**: the accept or dismiss gets 401 and the existing session-expiry handling sends the clinician to the login page.
- **Simulator saves many readings quickly**: each saved reading is evaluated for a crossing with the same rule; the no-duplicate rule (AC4) keeps at most one open suggestion per patient per urgency.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: When a reading is saved for an active patient and its stored risk band is strictly more urgent than the band of that patient's previous stored reading **and** the new band is one that raises a suggestion (`medium` or `high`, FR-002), the system MUST create exactly one open re-triage suggestion alert for the patient, built only from the stored score (`reading_scores`): it MUST NOT recompute NEWS2. The comparison MUST use a fixed band order from least to most urgent: `low` < `low-medium` < `medium` < `high`.
- **FR-002**: The system MUST map the new risk band to the alert severity (the `alerts.severity` values) in one place: only `medium` → `medium` and `high` → `high` raise a suggestion; the `low` and `low-medium` bands never raise a suggestion. The mapping MUST live in one backend place so it can be read in tests, and the glossary MUST record it (risk band and alert severity are separate concepts, Resolved name #4).
- **FR-003**: The system MUST map the suggesting risk band to how many triage levels more urgent to suggest, in the same place: `medium` → 1 level, `high` → 2 levels. The suggested level MUST be the patient's current level minus that step, clamped so it is never more urgent than Triage I (level 1); a patient already at Triage I MUST NOT receive a suggestion.
- **FR-004**: The suggestion alert's `message` MUST name the new risk band, the current triage level and the suggested triage level, and MUST NOT contain a raw NEWS2 computation; it MUST be safe, fixed-shape text with no submitted values or internal ids. The **reason** (the vital signs and points from the stored explanation entries, constitution Principle V) MUST be carried as **structured data** (parameter code + integer points, see FR-021), not as a pre-built human sentence, so the frontend can translate it (CR2).
- **FR-005**: Creating a suggestion alert MUST NOT change the patient's `triage_level` and MUST NOT create a `triage_event` with `source` = `automatic`; the app only suggests re-triage (constitution, D-41). The `source` = `automatic` enum value MUST stay in the schema unused by this story.
- **FR-006**: The raising risk band MUST be recorded in the suggestion alert's stored text (its `message`/`reason`, no new column) and read back to compare urgency. The system MUST NOT create a second open suggestion alert for a patient who already has one open suggestion unless a later reading's band is strictly more urgent than the recorded band of that open alert (that is, `medium` → `high`); in that case it MUST raise a new suggestion for the more urgent level. At most one open suggestion per patient MUST exist for a given urgency.
- **FR-007**: The system MUST offer a read operation that returns a patient's re-triage suggestion alerts (newest first) to any signed-in clinician (doctor or nurse; 401 otherwise), each with its severity, message, reason, current and suggested triage level and `status`; 404 for an unknown patient code or a code not in the patient code format. It MUST also answer for `discharged` patients.
- **FR-008**: The system MUST offer an accept operation that, for an open suggestion alert of a patient, in one transaction: sets the patient's `triage_level` to the suggested level, creates one `triage_event` (`source` = `manual`, `previous_level` the level before, `new_level` the suggested level, `created_by_id` the acting user, `occurred_at` the server save time, `reason` recording the accepted suggestion), and sets the alert to `acknowledged` with `acknowledged_by_id` and `acknowledged_at`. It MUST require a signed-in user (401 otherwise) whose role is `doctor` (403 for any other role, with a fixed message that only doctors can change the triage level), answer 404 for an unknown alert or patient, 409 when the alert is already acknowledged or the patient is `discharged`, and store nothing on any error.
- **FR-009**: The system MUST offer a dismiss operation that sets an open suggestion alert to `acknowledged` (with `acknowledged_by_id` and `acknowledged_at`) without changing the triage level or creating a triage event. It MUST require a signed-in user (401 otherwise) whose role is `doctor` (403 for any other role), answer 404 for an unknown alert or patient and 409 for an already acknowledged alert, and store nothing on any error.
- **FR-010**: All reads and writes in this story MUST read the stored risk band, score and explanation entries from `reading_scores` and MUST NOT import or depend on NEWS2 rules; any scoring access MUST go only through `app.scoring` (NEWS2 rules stay only in `app/scoring/news2.py`).
- **FR-011**: Error answers (401, 404, 409, 422 for a malformed body, and 503 when the database is unavailable, as in the existing endpoints) MUST use fixed, safe messages that reveal no internal details (no stack traces, database errors, submitted values or internal ids).
- **FR-012**: The app MUST provide a suggestion component mounted on the patient detail area with one line (so US-05 only keeps that line) that, for a patient with an open re-triage suggestion, shows inside the shared Card: the risk band as a `RiskBandBadge`, the current and suggested triage levels as `TriageLevelBadge`, the alert severity and the reason for each point. The "Accept" and "Dismiss" actions (shared Button) MUST be shown only when the signed-in user's role is `doctor`; a nurse sees the suggestion read-only with no action buttons. When there is no open suggestion it MUST show a translated "no open re-triage suggestion" message and no actions. The frontend MUST NOT compute a band, a score, a severity or a suggested level.
- **FR-013**: The accept and dismiss buttons MUST be disabled while their action is in progress. On success, Accept MUST show the patient's new triage level and that the suggestion was accepted; Dismiss MUST show that it was dismissed. On 404, 409, 5xx or a network/timeout failure the component MUST keep the suggestion shown and show a fixed, translated error message (never raw server text) with the buttons usable again; on 401 the existing session-expiry handling applies.
- **FR-014**: Every UI text in this story MUST be in Spanish by default and English with the language switch, come from the translations file and use glossary terms. The glossary MUST gain the new terms in the same PR (at least: the re-triage suggestion, the suggestion component, the Accept and Dismiss actions, the suggested triage level and the band→severity and band→step mapping).
- **FR-015**: All styling MUST use design tokens only (no raw colors outside `tokens.css`); color MUST NOT be the only cue (each badge keeps its label and icon); any new icon MUST come from `lucide-react` (D-46); the existing raw-color and contrast guard tests MUST keep passing.
- **FR-016**: Automated tests MUST cover: the band-crossing rule (crossing up into `medium`/`high` raises, crossing up into `low-medium` does not, same or down does not, first reading does not); the band→severity (`medium`→`medium`, `high`→`high`) and band→step (`medium`→1, `high`→2) mappings including the Triage I clamp and a patient already at Triage I; the no-duplicate rule, including a `medium` open alert that a later `high` reading replaces with a new one; accept (triage level set, one `manual` triage event, alert acknowledged, all-or-nothing) and dismiss (acknowledged, no triage event, no level change); the doctor-only check on accept and dismiss (nurse → 403); each API status (200/201 as applicable, 401, 403, 404, 409, 422, and discharged 409); the discharged and already-acknowledged edge cases; and the component (open suggestion view in both languages, doctor sees action buttons and nurse does not, no-suggestion view, accept and dismiss success, failed action keeps the suggestion, buttons disabled while in progress). All existing tests MUST pass, and `pytest`, `npm test`, `npm run build` and `npm audit --audit-level=high` MUST pass.
- **FR-017**: This story MUST NOT add a database migration: the existing `alerts` and `triage_events` tables are used as they are. The decision to map a risk band crossing to a re-triage suggestion alert (never changing the triage level on its own), the band→severity and band→step mappings, the no-duplicate rule and the accept/dismiss operations MUST be recorded as D-51 in `docs/DECISIONS.md`; `docs/BACKLOG.md` and `docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md` MUST show US-04 as done when the story is finished. The 2026-11-21 change request (CR1 list-page banner and `GET /api/suggestions`; CR2 structured translatable reason) MUST be recorded in the same D-51 entry.
- **FR-018** (CR1): The system MUST offer `GET /api/suggestions` returning every **open** re-triage suggestion across all patients to any signed-in clinician (401 otherwise), newest first (`created_at` desc, then `id` desc), each item carrying the **patient code**, the severity, the suggested triage level and the translatable reason data (FR-021). It MUST NOT return `patient_id`, `acknowledged_by_id` or the internal machine tag. A database error gives 503 with the fixed "Service unavailable" message.
- **FR-019** (CR1): The app MUST show, at the **top of the patient list page** (`/patients`), a banner of the open suggestions from FR-018, mounted as a self-contained component with one line (so US-02 only keeps that line when it builds the list). Each row MUST show the patient code, the severity as a colored badge with a `lucide-react` icon and a text label (**`high` → `--color-critical`/`--color-critical-soft`, red; `medium` → `--color-warning`/`--color-warning-soft`, yellow**; color never the only cue), and the suggested triage level, and MUST link to `/patients/<code>`. It MUST NOT offer accept or dismiss. On an empty result it MUST show a translated "no open suggestions" message (or render nothing), never an error; on a load error it MUST show a fixed translated message and no rows.
- **FR-020** (CR1): The banner MUST use design tokens only (reusing the existing warning/critical tokens; **no new token**), keep color off the only-cue path (icon + text label always shown), use `lucide-react` icons, and keep the raw-color and contrast guard tests green. Every text MUST be Spanish by default and English with the switch, from `translations.js`, using glossary terms.
- **FR-021** (CR2): The suggestion **reason** MUST be produced and carried as **structured, language-neutral data**: a list of entries `{parameter, points}` where `parameter` is the parameter code and `points` the integer points, including only parameters that scored > 0, in the standard `PARAMETERS` order, derived from the triggering reading's stored explanation entries (US-03) and never containing a submitted vital value. The API `reason` field (on both the per-patient suggestions and `GET /api/suggestions`) MUST be this list. The frontend MUST render the reason text from `parameter.*` and `recordReading.points` in `translations.js`, so it shows in the selected language with no English parameter code or bare number surfaced to the user. The stored `alerts.message` MUST NOT bake a human reason sentence into the database; the raising band and suggested level stay in the machine tag as before.

### Key Entities *(include if feature involves data)*

- **Re-triage suggestion alert**: an `alerts` row that suggests a more urgent triage level for a patient. Key attributes used by this story: `patient_id`, `severity` (`medium` or `high`, mapped from the raising risk band), `message` (names the raising band, the current level and the suggested level), `status` (`open` or `acknowledged`), `acknowledged_by_id`, `acknowledged_at`. The raising band, the suggested level and the reason are carried in the message/reason text built at creation time; the raising band is read back from that text for the no-duplicate comparison (FR-006). No new column is added.
- **Triage event (manual)**: a `triage_events` row created only when a clinician accepts a suggestion: `source` = `manual`, `previous_level`, `new_level` (the suggested level), `created_by_id`, `occurred_at`, `reason`.
- **Stored score (read-only here)**: the `reading_scores` row of a reading (US-03), read for its `band`, `total` and explanation `entries`; never recomputed.
- **Patient (read and update)**: `triage_level` is read to compute the suggested level and updated only on accept; `status` gates whether a suggestion is created or an accept is allowed.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: In 100% of saved readings whose risk band becomes more urgent than the patient's previous reading, exactly one open re-triage suggestion alert exists afterwards, and in 100% of readings that do not cross up, none is created.
- **SC-002**: In 100% of raised suggestions, the patient's triage level is unchanged until a clinician accepts, and no automatic triage event is ever created.
- **SC-003**: A clinician can read a patient's open suggestion, see why it was raised (the vital signs and points) and accept or dismiss it in under 1 minute, with the triage level changing only on accept.
- **SC-004**: 100% of error responses in this story carry a fixed, safe message with no submitted value, internal id or stack trace, and the full backend and frontend suites (including the raw-color and contrast guards) pass in CI.

## Assumptions

- The risk band order from least to most urgent is `low` < `low-medium` < `medium` < `high` (the four `RISK_BANDS` of US-03); a crossing means the latest reading's band is strictly more urgent than the previous stored reading's band. Only a crossing whose new band is `medium` or `high` raises a suggestion (Clarifications 2026-11-21); crossings into `low-medium` do not.
- "The threshold" in the story is the point where the band becomes more urgent than before (reaching `medium` or `high`); there is no separate numeric NEWS2 threshold, because the band already encodes the risk level and US-03 forbids recomputing NEWS2 outside the scoring module.
- Reusing the existing `alerts` and `triage_events` tables is enough; the raising band, the suggested level and the reason fit in the existing `message`/`reason` text, so no migration is needed (constitution Principle XII). No code creates any `Alert` row before this story, so US-04 defines the alert content without a prior model to match.

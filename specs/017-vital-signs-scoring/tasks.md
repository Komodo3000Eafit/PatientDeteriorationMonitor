## Phase 3: US-03 Save and score vital-sign readings 🎯 MVP

**Goal**: Every reading (simulator or nurse) is stored with exactly one NEWS2 result (system, NEWS2 score, risk band, 7 explanation entries); existing readings are backfilled; nurses enter readings on `/patients/<code>/readings/new` and see the stored score with the reason for every point; any signed-in clinician can read the history.

**Independent Test**: `python -m pytest` and `npm test` pass with the new tests; on a Neon branch `alembic upgrade head` leaves no reading without a score; the demo nurse enters the contract example for `PAT-001` and sees "Puntaje NEWS2 5", "Banda de riesgo: Media" and seven reasons; the demo doctor sees no button and the nurses-only message ([quickstart.md](quickstart.md) §2–§5).
